# Reviewer guide

[Launch Fynura](https://fynura-g7sjcbc4ua-uc.a.run.app/welcome) and select **Continue with Google**. A Google account, including Gmail, is supported; no separate Fynura password is required. Organizational account policies may restrict access.

## Suggested walkthrough

1. Explore the global map and change threat, WHO region and metric.
2. Inspect a country observation, reporting cutoff and original source.
3. Compare historical reports and inspect point labels, coverage and downloadable data.
4. Ask “What is herd immunity, and why does it matter for measles?” without selecting chart context.
5. Continue with a follow-up and inspect the linked evidence.
6. Use a chart's Ask action for a question about that specific visual.
7. Generate a brief or infographic and inspect its source links.

See the [detailed testing instructions](TESTING_INSTRUCTIONS.md), [project story](DEVPOST_PROJECT_DETAILS.md), [architecture](ARCHITECTURE_EXPLANATION.md) and [gallery](GALLERY_PLAN.md).

Research availability and latency depend on source and provider availability. The interface displays progress and explicit errors. Stored surveillance confidence and research evidence support describe different aspects of evidence quality, not guarantees of correctness.

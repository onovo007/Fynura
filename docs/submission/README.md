## Product scope

Structured surveillance covers selected WHO measles, cholera and Ebola reports. Historical archives provide additional WHO, CDC and OWID-derived context. Ask Fynura researches questions with Gemini on Vertex AI and Google Search, with linked sources and optional chart context.

Reporting periods, geographic scope and source limitations remain visible. Historical screening is exploratory, not an outbreak forecast. Fynura supports public-health understanding, not individual diagnosis or treatment.

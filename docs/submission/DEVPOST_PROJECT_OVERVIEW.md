# Fynura

**See the signal sooner.**

Fynura turns fragmented public-health evidence into traceable intelligence through automated surveillance processing, agent-led research and source-linked explanations.

Designed for public-health professionals, researchers, journalists and communities, Fynura connects reported observations to geographic views, historical context and an interactive research partner.

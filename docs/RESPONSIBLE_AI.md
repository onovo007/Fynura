# Responsible AI

Fynura prefers event-specific national/international health authorities, attaches a source URL and timestamps to every observation, and exposes conflicts rather than averaging them away. Deterministic code owns arithmetic and evidence selection. LLM briefing is constrained to verified structured evidence.

The MVP stores no PHI or patient-level data. It is public-health situational intelligence, not diagnosis, treatment advice, causal inference, or an individual-risk predictor. Reporting delays, changing definitions, under-reporting, and revisions are always material limitations. Missing reports never mean zero cases. Bounded source excerpts are retained only for auditability.
